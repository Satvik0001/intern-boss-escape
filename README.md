# Intern Boss Escape 🏃‍♂️💼

**Intern Boss Escape** is a 2D stealth gameplay prototype built in **Unity and C#**.

The player takes the role of an intern who has one simple objective:

> **Escape the office without getting caught by the manager.**

The manager patrols the office and has a limited field of vision. The player needs to observe the manager's movement, avoid entering their line of sight, and carefully make their way toward the exit.

## 🎮 Gameplay

The player navigates through an office environment while trying to stay out of the manager's vision.

The challenge comes from understanding the manager's patrol pattern and choosing the right moment to move.

If the manager spots the intern, the player is caught and the attempt ends.

**Get out of the office without getting caught.**
